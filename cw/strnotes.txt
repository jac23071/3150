String methods:
	-contains (in c23 only)
	-substr(idx, num_chars_after_idx)
	-size_t(use for idx in for loops)
	-npos (no position found; in size_t type)
	-toupper and tolower (modifies strings char by char; part of std namespace)
	-isalpha
	-isdigit
	-isspace
	-handling strings and ints:
		+stoi (string to int)
		+itos (int to string)
			*to_string([num])
		+stod (string to double)
		+dtos (double to string)
Range-based for loops: for([type][name]:[buffer]){}
	-each element is passed by value, not by reference. (each element is a copy)
